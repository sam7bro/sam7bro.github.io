Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2025

SKU Campus Companion ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Profile Information: Full name, email address, phone number, branch, year of study, gender
  • Account Credentials: Login information for authentication
  • User Content: YouTube channel links, place descriptions you edit

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Location Data: GPS coordinates when using navigation features (only while app is in use)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features accessed, time spent, interaction patterns
  • Camera Access: Only when you use the Geo Camera feature

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Firebase Services: Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Messaging
  • Google AdMob: Advertising identifiers (if ads are enabled)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Enable campus navigation and location services
  • Send push notifications about campus announcements
  • Process emergency SOS requests
  • Improve user experience and app performance
  • Display relevant advertisements (if applicable)
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests

3. Information Sharing

We DO NOT sell your personal information.

We may share information:

  • With Campus Security: Location data during SOS activation only
  • With Service Providers: Firebase (Google), AdMob for app functionality
  • For Legal Compliance: When required by law or to protect rights
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Firebase Security Rules for database access
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security assessments

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

  • Profile Data: Retained while account is active
  • Location Data: Not stored permanently; used in real-time only
  • Usage Analytics: Retained for up to 12 months
  • Deleted Accounts: Data removed within 30 days of deletion request

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request account and data deletion
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications or location services
  • Portability: Request data in portable format

To exercise these rights, contact: sameerbaba03@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for university students (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.
