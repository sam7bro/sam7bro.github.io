Description of Service

SKU Campus Companion is a campus navigation and information platform designed for Sri Krishnadevaraya University (SKU) students, faculty, and visitors. The service provides:

  • Campus navigation with real-time GPS and turn-by-turn directions
  • Information about listed places including academic buildings, hostels, libraries, cafeterias, banks, hospitals, and sports facilities
  • Smart Geo Camera for location-based photo detection
  • YouTube Channels to discover and share campus content
  • Emergency SOS with instant location sharing
  • Emergency Contacts for hospitals, hostels, and security
  • Push Notifications for important announcements
  • QR code generation for easy location sharing
