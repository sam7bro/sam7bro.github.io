Delete Account

Learn how to permanently delete your SKU Campus Companion account and what happens to your data.

calendar_today Last Updated: September 2025 schedule ~4 min read warning Permanent Action
warning
Account deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your personal data cannot be recovered. This is different from logging out.
1

Delete from inside the app

The fastest and recommended way to delete your account:

1

Open the app

Sign in with the account you want to delete.

2

Go to App Info

Open the side menu and tap App Info.

3

Scroll to the Account section

At the bottom you will see the Delete Account option.

4

Confirm and delete

Read the information, tick the confirmation box, type DELETE, and tap Delete My Account. If you signed in with Google, you may be asked to sign in again for security.

2

Delete via email request

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email. We will verify the request using your registered email address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Send an email from your registered account email address to:

sameerbaba03@gmail.com

Include the following in the email:

  • The email address you signed up with
  • Your SKU UID (found in Profile → SKU UID), if available
  • The subject line: Account Deletion Request
3

What is deleted

  • Your account record (name, email, phone, branch, year, gender)
  • Push notifications targeted specifically at your account
  • Your saved login session and push-notification token on this device
  • Your Firebase Authentication account (if you signed up with Google or email)
4

What is retained (and why)

Some content must be retained for the app to function safely for other students:

  • YouTube channels you added remain visible to other students. Your display name is retained as creator credit. All other personal data linked to the channel is removed. To request removal of the retained credit name, email sameerbaba03@gmail.com.
  • Admin audit logs (if you were an admin) are retained for security and legal purposes. These logs contain only your name, the action performed, and a timestamp.
  • Campus places, app info, and broadcast notifications are public content and are not affected by your account deletion.
5

Timeline & confirmation

  • In-app deletion: immediate — you will be signed out right away.
  • Email requests: completed within 30 days of verification.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.
6

Frequently asked

Is logout the same as account deletion?

No. Logout only ends your session — your account and data remain. Deletion permanently removes your account and personal data.

Can I recover my deleted account?

No. Deletion is permanent. You may create a new account later with the same email if you wish.

Will deleting my account remove my YouTube channel from the app?

No — the channel remains visible to other students, and your display name is retained as creator credit. All other personal data is removed. You may request removal of the retained credit name by emailing us.

Why are admin audit logs retained?

They help us maintain the security and integrity of the app. They contain no personal content beyond your name, the action performed, and a timestamp.
